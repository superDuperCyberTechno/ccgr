## Ello
*v0.10.1*

Originally based on *Oh Hell!*, tweaked into something different. Most notably this game features an optional automa represented by the stock (to make it viable as a 2-player game), fixed hand size, new scoring and a new can-follow mechanic ([read this](?p=articles-core_values)).

### Requirements
2-6 players. A standard deck of 52 cards.

### Goal
Accrue most points.

### Setup
All players receive 7 cards. The rest of the cards are put aside as stock (placed between 2 players) with the top card flipped and put below the stock to denote the trump suit.

### Play
Clockwise, one at a time, each participant plays 1 card. The winner of the trick is determined as follows: Highest card of the trump suit wins the trick, else, highest card of the leading suit wins the trick.

Cards are played in 2 possible states: Face-up or face-down. Cards can only be played face-up if they are capable of winning the trick.
If any player can't or won't play a card that will win the current trick state, a card is chosen at random from their hand and played face down. Any card played like this cannot win the trick. In other words, the last played face-up card wins the trick.

After all players have played a card, all face-down cards played *after* the last face-up card will be awarded to the winner as points, 1 point per card. E.g., if the second player wins the trick in a 5-player game, they get 3 face-down cards which will count as points (3 cards were played after the last winning card). If the last player won the trick, they get 0 points (no face-down cards played afterwards).

The winner of each trick leads the next.

The stock is treated as a player and will get a turn, based on its position on the table. On its turn, play its top card. Check if the revealed card is winning the trick and play it face-down if not. Any points won by the stock are discarded. If the stock is leading a trick, simply play the top card face-up.
